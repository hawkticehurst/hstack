# Prototype brief template

Write one brief per chosen direction, in the topic's `briefs/` folder. Someone who has never seen the topic should be able to build the right prototype from the brief alone. Link ids to the views so a reader can check the evidence, but never rely on the reader following a link.

```markdown
# <Direction name> (<V-id>)

## The question

<The one question this prototype answers, for example "Can someone who runs four sessions tell which one needs them, without opening any?" One question, so the result is clear.>

## The bet

<The direction in two or three sentences: Which objects lead, how the information is organized, who acts, and where it lives.>

## Who and when

<The audience (A-id) and the situation, in their own words. The target needs (N-ids) as job stories.>

## Build

<The breadboard: Places, affordances, and connections. The states to cover: First use, empty, many, the target situation, error, and done.>

## Real and fake

- Must be real: <the part the question depends on. Never fake the thing being tested.>
- Can be faked: <data, back ends, and flows outside the question>
- Content: <real names and words from the glossary and the evidence, never placeholder text>
- Host: <for an existing product, inside the real surface, or a faithful copy of it; for several directions, side by side in one prototype so people can compare>

## Success and failure

- Supports the direction: <what you would see>
- Rules it out: <what you would see>
- Riskiest assumption it tests: <H-id and text>

## Out of scope

<No-gos and rabbit holes from the direction, and anything to leave rough.>

## Evidence

<The few quotes the direction rests on most, with their E-ids, so the prototype stays honest to them.>
```
